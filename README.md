# Pundo

Pundo is a mobile-first pilot for Filipinos working abroad.

The current version starts with Filipino helpers in Singapore and helps them move from:

**my life today → where my money goes → what I want → my house → my community → what I want to build**

## What it does

- Starts with current income, remittance, debt, savings, and family costs.
- Lets helpers choose where they would seriously consider building or owning something, including Davao, Cebu, Bacolod, or somewhere else.
- Lets them configure a possible home: rent, rent-to-own, ownership, bedrooms, bathrooms, family size, and realistic monthly housing budget.
- Asks which shared community needs actually matter, such as healthcare, education, internet, backup power, childcare, and small business space.
- Uses conversational Tagalog/Taglish under the questions.
- Includes a privacy warning not to share bank/card numbers, passwords, OTPs, Singpass details, passport/FIN numbers, employer addresses, or other private information.
- Does not store form responses on the website.
- Opens WhatsApp with completed answers prefilled.
- Supports Messenger as a secondary option by copying the completed answers for the user to paste into the chat.
