# Pundo landing page

Single-file, mobile-first landing page for the Pundo helper project.

## What it does
- Focuses version 1 on Filipino domestic helpers already working in Singapore.
- Avoids showing proposed Pundo services before the survey so responses are not primed by our ideas.
- Uses a short 4-step form focused on actual behavior and real amounts: years worked, employers, duties, pay, padala, debt, savings, remittance frequency and fees, running short before payday, expensive purchases/services, switching behavior, pooling with other helpers, and what working in Singapore has already built.
- Uses conversational Tagalog/Taglish only under the questions.
- Includes a privacy warning not to share bank/card numbers, passwords, OTPs, Singpass, passport/FIN numbers, employer addresses, or other private information.
- Does not store form responses on the website.
- Opens WhatsApp with the answers prefilled. The helper decides whether to send them.
