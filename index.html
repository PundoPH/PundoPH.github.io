Tell us about you

Why Pundo exists

You send money home every month. After years of doing that, what do you actually have left for yourself?

Domestic work is real work. Pundo is starting with Filipino helpers already working in Singapore. We want to understand the real numbers behind the work: salary, remittance, debt, savings, emergencies, and what you want to have after years of working abroad.

What we are trying to learn

Not “would this be nice?” We want to know what is actually happening.

We are not starting with a list of services and asking you to say yes. We are starting with your real experience in Singapore.

Your work

How long you have worked here, how many employers you have had, and what work you actually do.

Your money

Salary, how much goes home, what remains, debt, savings, and what happens when an emergency comes.

Your costs

How you send money home and what helpers in Singapore may be paying too much for.

Your future

What you want after 3 to 5 more years of work, and what would actually help you get there.

What Pundo may test later

Only if helpers tell us the problem is real. These are not services we already offer.

  • Finding the better remittance rate and lower fee when helpers send money home
  • Group pricing for medical care or other things helpers already pay for
  • A simple way to put money aside toward a house, land, business, or another goal
  • Other shared services where helpers can get a better deal by acting together
